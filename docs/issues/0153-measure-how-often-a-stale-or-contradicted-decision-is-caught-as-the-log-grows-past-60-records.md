## 0153. Measure how often a stale or contradicted decision is caught as the log grows past 60 records

An outside comment, 2026-11-23: "the model noticing a contradiction works until it doesn't, and then nobody knows a decision went stale; how does it hold up once the log gets past 60 or so?" This bundle holds 103 ADRs, 59 in force. A design review the same day found that no deterministic signal catches a contradiction between two decisions in force, and that every stale decision of that session was caught by a person or an agent reading, never by the tool.

### Scope

- Measure, with the existing eval harness (`evals/`), how often an agent catches a contradiction it should resolve when the bundle holds 60 and 240 ADRs, before building anything.
- After the measurement, and only if its decision rule says so: candidate records printed by `write new adr` (O2), and a declared amend relation (O1).

Kept: `write supersede` and its chain checks; the determinism boundary (no model inside the binary).

### Decision

- Failure taxonomy (design review, 2026-11-23): T1 contradicted by a newer decision with no supersede; T2 partially reversed with no supersede (supersede retires a whole record, so a partial reversal is written as prose, as ADR 0099 did against ADR 0027); T3 contradicted by code; T4 never implemented; T5 premise invalidated by the world. Today `check` catches part of T3 and T4 (`path-missing`, `stale-view`) and none of T1, T2 or T5. Supersede closes a declared, whole replacement only.
- Measure first (owner, 2026-11-23): the scale eval (O4) runs before any build; then candidates at `write new adr` (O2) and a declared amend relation (O1) as the measurement decides. Not taken: building O2 and O1 at once; adding the `verification-unbound` advisory (O3) now; a scope-overlap advisory (O6, the DUPLICATE pass ADR 0057 cut); a `reviewed:` date on every record (O7).
- Decision rule, fixed before running: if the unprompted catch rate at 240 ADRs is below the prompted arm's, or below the unprompted rate at 60 ADRs, build O2 and rerun the same scenarios against it; then decide O1 from the T2 numbers.
- After the measurement (owner, 2026-11-23): the rule says build O2, so an advisor designs how `write new adr` finds candidates deterministically before an ADR; O1 waits. Not taken: deciding O1 now; stopping at the measurement.
- O2 is measured in variants, smallest first (owner, 2026-11-23, after a design review): the transcripts show unprompted sessions already ran `read records --topic`, got an empty answer on a paraphrased conflict and read it as no conflict. V1 rewords the empty `--topic` answer (records in force, no word match is not evidence of no conflict, `read records` lists them); V2 adds one fixed line to `write new adr` asking whether the decision contradicts or partly reverses a record in force; V3 (candidates by rare shared terms) is built only if V1 and V2 fall short. Rule fixed before running: keep the smallest variant whose Sonnet unprompted paraphrased catch reaches 11/12 with no false alarm and no over-link. The scorer first separates superseded from merely named and counts a non-plant link as over-linked, and noise controls are added. Not taken: V1 alone; V3 directly (candidates on 84 to 94 of 94 writes over this repository's ADRs); pausing O2.
- After measuring V1 and V2 (owner, 2026-11-24): only a supersede of the noise ADR is a false alarm in a noise control; a new record that names it as context and leaves it in force is reported as over-linked, for information. The scorer changes before V3, and V1 and V2 are rescored from their raw outputs with no new model run. Not taken: counting any citation as a false alarm.
- V3 is built on V2 (owner, 2026-11-24): neither V1 (6/12) nor V2 (9/12) reached the fixed 11/12, so `write new adr` adds candidate in-force ADRs that share rare terms with the new title, description and body, on top of V2's question, by ADR before code, then measured as V1 and V2 were. Not taken: keeping V2 and stopping, which would change the rule after seeing the data; a full V2 rerun first; stopping O2.

Elsewhere a link to a non-plant record still fails it. Not taken: the clause applying to the noise controls too, which every variant failed 6/6 by citing related context.
- O2 closes on V3 (owner, 2026-11-24): V3 met the fixed rule (11/12, no false alarm, no over-link outside the noise controls), so ADR 0105 and ADR 0106 are accepted and V1, V2 and V3 ship together. Not taken: a full 120-run V3 rerun first; deciding O1 now (S9 stays open).
- S9 follows a staged plan, measured before building (owner, 2026-11-24, after a design review): stage 0 fixes the scorer (the plant's successor must be a record added in the run; each surviving clause, seeded with a unique token, must stay in force) and adds cross-topic exception scenarios; stage 1 measures R0 (today), R1 (`read records` shows later in-force ADRs whose Decision links a record) and R2 (partial supersede) smallest first, and S9 closes unbuilt if R0 meets its rule; stage 2 counts, then measures, `read records --path` and the binding of a decision to the test that proves it (T3, T4); stage 3 measures an opt-in `revisit` date and a `Challenges [ADR]` convention (T5). Not taken: building the partial supersede first; pausing S9; a general relation model, RDF, a store, blanket review dates, `covers:` globs or git-history signals in the binary.

Not taken: a partial supersede for every partial reversal.
- `check` may have one clock-dependent rule (owner, 2026-11-24): an opt-in `revisit: <date>` on a record that names an external premise raises an advisory once due, with `--as-of <date>` making the run reproducible; it ships only if stage 3's measurement keeps it. Not taken: no clock in `check`; deciding at stage 3.

Not taken: three repetitions with point rates.

Not taken: calibrating lab rates against field sessions first; building S11 and S12 as planned; closing the issue here; the decision-to-test binding in `check` first, or both S11 features at once; only the `Challenges [ADR]` convention, both T5 mechanisms, or leaving T5 out.
- S11's count rule, fixed before counting (owner, 2026-11-24): over the ADRs in force of this repository and two private bundles (published only as aggregate counts, never by name), a deterministic script flags T3 when a file named in Implementation impact changed in 3 or more commits after the ADR's timestamp and no newer record names that file; T4 when no file named in Implementation impact has a commit after the ADR's timestamp; T5 when the body names a version, price, quota or vendor limit. A hand read of up to 10 candidates per type estimates precision; a type counts as frequent when candidates × precision reach at least 10% of the ADRs in force, and only a frequent type gets its feature. Not taken: counting this repository alone; a 5% or a 20% threshold.
- S11 rule clarified before any counted run (owner, 2026-11-24, after a review and an advisor read of the pilot): a directory or glob named in Implementation impact expands to its files and the rule applies per file; precision comes from a seeded sample of up to 10 candidates per type read by the session, and a type is frequent when candidates × the Wilson lower bound of that precision reach 10% of the ADRs in force pooled over the three bundles; the implementing commit counting towards T3 and clearing T4 stays as written and is reported as a limit. Instrument fixes that only make the script read what the rule says (the whole Implementation impact field, brace expansion, crate-relative paths resolved by unique suffix or reported unresolved, commit filtering that does not stop at skewed dates, timestamps compared as instants with a missing one excluded and counted, a recall-biased version pattern) land with tests, and the count runs only from a committed script named by hash. Not taken: a directory or glob as one unit, or excluding them; the point estimate; per-bundle thresholds.
- A local classifier is measured offline before any ADR (owner, 2026-11-24, after an advisor read): the CLI keeps ADR 0106's lexical candidates; an eval-only replay under `evals/` ranks in-force ADRs for at least 40 seeded paraphrased plants with no shared rare word (plus controls) with ADR 0106, BM25, a static embedding table (potion-base-8M, integer arithmetic) and all-MiniLM-L6-v2 as a ceiling; an ADR for the static table is written only if its recall at 3 has a 95% Wilson interval wholly above the lexical one with no more candidates per write on the controls. An in-process encoder or NLI judge is out under any result, since it would make the tool decide (ADR 0001). Not taken: writing the static-table ADR now; an encoder or NLI inside the CLI; dropping the idea.
- What "a newer record names that file" means for T3 (owner, 2026-11-24, after a second review showed this repository's T3 at 4, 28 or 5 under three readings, so this repository's numbers were seen before the choice; the two private bundles were not): the exact file path anywhere in a newer record's body names it; a directory or glob names the files under it only when it appears in the newer record's own Implementation impact field; globs follow git's pathspec semantics (`*` does not cross `/`), and a crate-relative token resolves by unique suffix on both sides. Not taken: exact path only; a directory or glob mentioned anywhere in the body.
- How S13's fixed rule is run (owner, 2026-11-25, before any S13 code or run): each ranker that scores every record (BM25, potion-base-8M, all-MiniLM-L6-v2) gets a score cut chosen on a calibration seed so that its candidates per write on the controls match the lexical finder's (ADR 0106), and recall at 3 is measured on a separate test seed with that cut frozen; the replay is Python run by `uv` under `evals/` with pinned dependencies, outside the workspace crates, and fetches the two models from Hugging Face on its first run; the 40 or more plants are a committed fixture of pairs (new ADR text, paraphrased plant) written by the session and reviewed before any run, with a script proving no pair shares a rare word under ADR 0106's tokenizer, inserted into the seed 153 bundles of 60 and 240 ADRs, and their model authorship is reported as a limit. Not taken: a score threshold fixed a priori; top 3 for every ranker, which drops the controls clause; a Rust eval binary; plants expanded from templates in `concerns.tsv`.

### Acceptance

- Ten scenarios (T1, T2 and a control with no plant; 60 and 240 ADRs; the conflicting record worded with the task's words and paraphrased), two arms (unprompted task; task that asks for conflicting record numbers in `CONFLICTS.txt`), two models, three runs each, scored by `expect.sh`: caught means the planted record is superseded by or linked from the new record (unprompted) or listed (prompted); the control fails on any supersede or listed number.
- An aggregate over the runs' `score.json`: catch and false-alarm rate per type, size, wording, arm and model, plus `read_before_first_write`, recorded in this issue's Outcome with the cost of the runs.
- The scenario generator is seeded and deterministic; the binary is not changed.
